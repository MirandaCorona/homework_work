Do YOU have a toddler?

Two year olds

I love my toddler, even though raising her is like living in a tornado. She is the light of my life, who also drives me crazy.

The drawing on the walls, screaming, crying, inability to accept the word no, and their unspoken perfect timing of kisses.

I will also admit it heals me watching her run down the hall after a long shift at work just to greet me and ask if I am happy.

Going on three, more like going on thirteen

It is amazing how much they pick up from us without us knowing. One day she is my sweet sleeping bundle of joy and another she speaks in full sentences, with cold hearted attitude at times. She reminds me of the best parts of both myself and my husband. The ability to insist she is helping while doing something extremely unhelpful is bizarre. The "I can do it myself" moments when i just dont have time. Children teach you to slow down, they teach you patience. I often find myself asking, "Why is she growing up so fast?"

And DONT forget about the endless why questions?

  • Why is the sky blue?
  • Are you happy momma?
  • Are you frustrated?
  • Can I go outside?
  • You love me?
  • What is that noise?
  • Can you help me?
  • What's in your mouth?
  • Can I have some juice?

Sometimes, they drive you mad. Other times they bring you joy. This is why I believe raising toddlers are like living in a tornado. You never know what to expect, you just hang on for dear life and pray you dont mess everything up.

No matter how overwhelmed I get, I wouldn't trade these moments for anything. Sometimes I feel like I'm drowning in chaos, but then I see her smile and it all makes sense.
It is okay to feel exhausted or overwhelmed. You are still a good mother or father, just doing their best with what they have and that is enough.
I am so grateful for my motherhood journey, even on its hardest days. Especially on the days where I just feel like giving up, I just remind myself SHE is worth it.
